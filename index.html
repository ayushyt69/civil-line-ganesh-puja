Our Samiti

Faith • Unity • Celebration

✦

We are not just a committee; we are a family, united by faith, devotion, and the spirit of togetherness.

Civil Line Ganesh Puja Samiti brings our community together to celebrate Ganesh Puja with devotion, joy and harmony. Every celebration is made special by the people who contribute their time, effort and blessings.

🙏

Ganpati Bappa Morya!

May Lord Ganesha bless our family and community with wisdom, prosperity, happiness and unity.
